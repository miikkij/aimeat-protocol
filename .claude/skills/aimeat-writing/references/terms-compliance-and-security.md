# Language context: compliance, security and signatures

The decided words for secrets and the vault, content classification and audience, GDPR registers, requests and agreements, laws asked as questions, electronic signatures, provenance and AI marks, what the node reads from an item, the Data Governance Act.

Read `language-rules.md` first: it says what the decided words bind you to, which words are
forbidden, and how a row is added or changed. `language-context.md` lists the other area files.
