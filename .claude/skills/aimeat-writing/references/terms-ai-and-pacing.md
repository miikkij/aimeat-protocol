# Language context: AI models, morsels, goals and workflows

The decided words for morsels and the AI allowance, AI providers, models, roles and their settings, the decision model and its rules, image and speech terms, workflows, goals and the work agents take.

Read `language-rules.md` first: it says what the decided words bind you to, which words are
forbidden, and how a row is added or changed. `language-context.md` lists the other area files.
