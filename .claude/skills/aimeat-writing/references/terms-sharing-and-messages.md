# Language context: sharing, workspaces and messages

The decided words for organisms, teams and workspaces with their roles, spaces and sections, contacts, Messages and group chats, asking other members, boards, notifications and connected mailboxes.

Read `language-rules.md` first: it says what the decided words bind you to, which words are
forbidden, and how a row is added or changed. `language-context.md` lists the other area files.

| a shared group | people and AIs sharing work | organism | organismi | organismo |
| a shared container | documents and records inside an organism | workspace | työtila | espacio de trabajo |
| a rule on a key pattern that every account's writes under it must match | a schema (structure) lock: set by an account, its agent or app, or seeded by the node; its holder may change or remove it, and an erased account's lock names a pseudonym so no account holds it. Not the sign-in lockout, which is *Kirjautumisen lukitus* / *sign-in lockout* (the Security page's erased-lock-holders incident) | lock (schema lock) | lukitus | bloqueo |
| an organism, said to a newcomer | the home's first task and the guided path name an organism by what it does for them before the word organism appears: one place every AI they connect and every person they invite reads and writes. | asking to be let into an app or a page | a person an app, a welcome page or a company front page refuses, because it is opened to one shared place's members, asks from that page; the owners and admins of the place approve or reject, and approving makes the person a member. The organism's setting allows it or not. Finnish builds on the verb *pyytää pääsyä*; the compound *pääsypyyntö* is not in use (searched 2026-11-12) and is never written | access request; ask for access; allowed / not allowed | pääsyn pyytäminen; pyytää pääsyä; pyyntö; sallittu / ei sallittu | solicitud de acceso; pedir acceso; permitidas / no permitidas |

| what a team knows now | one record in the team's shared-knowledge workspace that every member's AI reads before it answers about the team: what the team does, who is in it, what is open | team context | tiimin konteksti | contexto del equipo |
| how a team works, for an AI | a skill only the team's members and their AIs read: where each thing is written and what waits for a person; a member proposes a change and a lead approves it | team skill | tiimiskilli | skill del equipo |
| which agents an organism lets in | the organism's setting: every member's agents, or only the agents listed in its Agents section; any other agent is treated as a non-member | admit (an agent) | päästää sisään (agentin) | admitir (un agente) |
| who may only read a workspace | a member given read access to one workspace; never *katselija* or *espectador* | viewer | lukija | lector |
| who may read and write a workspace | a member given write access to one workspace; never *osallistuja* or *contribuidor* | contributor | kirjoittaja | colaborador |
| who made a workspace | the person who created it; always has access, and approves members' changes with the admins | creator | luoja | quien lo creó |

| another person of a company node | on a server whose operator turned on that everyone sees each other, every other person with an account there: in the address book without anyone saving them, and their first message needs no approval; never an agent or an app | colleague | kollega | colega |
| one part of a workspace | a list of records or a set of document pages, declared in the workspace's structure | space | tila | espacio |
| a space an app keeps for its own tools | a record space whose structure names the app's extensions: a person and the app write it directly, an agent writes it only through one of the app's tools, unless its owner gave the agent the permission to write directly. In the permission sentences, where *shared space* already means the organism, it is "a part of a shared space" (*jaetun tilan osa*, *una parte de un espacio compartido*) | space reserved for the app's tools | sovelluksen työkaluille varattu tila | espacio reservado para las herramientas de la app |
| a heading pages are filed under | one entry of a document space's tree of sections; a page sits in one section or in none | section | osio | sección |
| a member's change that waits | a change a member who is neither the workspace's creator nor an admin made to its structure (a space, the sections), waiting until the creator or an admin approves or declines it | suggestion / suggest | ehdotus / ehdottaa | sugerencia / sugerir |
| how a workspace takes its members' changes | the workspace's own rule: members change it at once, or members suggest and an admin approves | member changes | jäsenten muutokset | cambios de los miembros |
| a space for what a group piles up | a workspace space for rows that keep arriving (messages, events, readings): appended, never edited, read with their own tools | row space | rivitila | espacio de filas |
| what turns a mailbox into rows | a batch that reads the next page of a connected mailbox, sorts each message into a kind and saves it as a row in a workspace, on a button, a schedule or the person's AI; the Finnish word is the one the aimeat-refinery skill is triggered by | mail refinery | postinjalostamo | procesador de correo |
| one thread in Messages | everything said between the same parties under one subject | conversation | keskustelu | conversación |
| where a person puts conversations away | nothing is deleted; a conversation comes back when someone other than their own agents writes | archive / archive (verb) | arkisto / arkistoida | archivo / archivar |
| the conversations with messages the person has not opened yet | the top section of the Messages list and the count beside a heading; a conversation leaves the section when the person opens it | unread | lukematta | sin leer |
| a rule for the Messages list | folds, groups or archives the conversations it matches; the first one that fits decides | rule | sääntö | regla |
| a conversation of several people a person opened | named by its name; everybody in it is on this node; never *organism*, which is shared knowledge, nor *sharing group*, which is a list for consent | group chat; group (in a list) | ryhmäkeskustelu; ryhmä | conversación de grupo; grupo |
| the one who adds and removes a group chat's members | whoever opened it, until they leave; then the next person in it | manages the group | ylläpitää ryhmää | administra el grupo |
| a symbol given on one message instead of a reply | an emoji or a short word that acknowledges the message; each person gives a symbol once and can take it back; the other side sees it at once. | the symbols a person offers first when reacting | their own list, each symbol with what it means, kept in their memory; an AI reads the meaning | palette | pikavalikko | paleta |
| being asked into a group chat | what a person gets when their contacts do not yet accept the one adding them; they see nothing of the group until they join | invitation; join / decline | kutsu; liity / kieltäydy | invitación; entrar / rechazar |
| someone in a group chat | a person or an agent who reads and writes in it | member | jäsen | miembro |
| asking the members of the same node who know | a member's question goes to members whose chosen topics match, as a message thread; they answer themselves or through their AI. | the member who answers | chose to be asked about their topics, within a weekly limit; helping keeps its warm word | helper; help | auttaja; auttaa | persona que ayuda; ayudar |

| an answer kept for the next person | the helper allowed it and the asker asked for it; the helper confirms it every few months, unconfirmed it is out of date | kept answer | säilytetty vastaus | respuesta guardada |
