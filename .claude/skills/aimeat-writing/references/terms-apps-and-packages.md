# Language context: apps, extensions and packages

The decided words for published apps and the marks on them, extensions, skills and app tools, what agents and apps offer, an app's look and design spec, visitors and what the AI label says, packages, the store and paid packages, tool servers.

Read `language-rules.md` first: it says what the decided words bind you to, which words are
forbidden, and how a row is added or changed. `language-context.md` lists the other area files.
