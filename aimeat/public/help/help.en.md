## Getting started {#start}

AIMEAT is your own place where AI does work for you. It remembers what you tell it, makes agents and apps for you, and everything in it is yours.

Start with one sentence. Open your [Home](/v1/home) and write in the field what you want done, for example:

> Gather the industry news every weekday morning and write a short summary.

AIMEAT makes an agent from it and shows it to you before it starts. You can also talk with the AI directly in the [chat](/v1/chat).

If you already use Claude, ChatGPT or another AI, you can connect it here. Then it sees the same information and does the same things as the chat. The instructions are on your Home.

## Who sees my things {#privacy}

Everything you save is private at first. Only you see it.

You decide what you share. You can share a workspace with the people you invite, or publish something for everyone. What you share is seen only by those you shared it with.

An agent or a connected AI may do only what you allow it. You see every permission, and can take it back, on the [Access page](/v1/profile?tab=access).

You can export all your data and delete your account in Settings.

## Agents and schedules {#agents}

An agent is an AI that does one job for you, also when you are away.

Say in one sentence on your [Home](/v1/home) what you want. AIMEAT makes the agent and shows it to you before it starts. If the work repeats, say so: for example "every weekday at 8 in the morning". Then the agent does the work on that schedule.

What the agent finishes shows on your Home. You can stop an agent at any time.

## Apps {#apps}

An app is a small web page that does one thing, for example a calculator, a form or a game.

Ask the AI to make an app, for example in the chat: "Make me an app to follow my customers' orders." The finished app opens at its own address, and its data is saved here.

## Signing in {#signin}

You sign in with an email link. Write your address in the sign-in window, and you get a link. The link works once and for 15 minutes, and you need no password.

If you want, you can add a passkey. Then you sign in with your fingerprint, your face or your device's screen lock. You add a passkey on the [Security page](/v1/profile?tab=security).

## If something breaks {#broken}

Most things are safe to try. Everything is private at first, and an agent does only what you allow it.

If something does not work, write to [support@aimeat.io](mailto:support@aimeat.io). Say what you tried to do and what happened. If your AI is connected here, it can send the message for you.

## Advanced: your own AI {#advanced}

You need this part only if you want to use an AI service of your own.

You can save your own AI key in the [AI settings](/v1/profile?tab=ai), for example an OpenRouter key. Then the AI use is billed to your own account at that service.

## Knowledge and packages {#knowledge}

Knowledge here is what people have refined from their own material: notes with their sources, gathered into a package. You can read packages others have published in the public library, and your AI can use them when it answers you.
