## Getting started {#start}

AIMEAT multiplies what your AI can do and makes it easy to use. ChatGPT, Claude or Grok remembers your work here, builds what you need and works with the people you choose. Agents do the agreed work all the time, also while you are away.

You decide who sees what and what an AI may do, and you see what it has done. Everything here is yours.

<!--store-->
Your [Home](/v1/home) has four first steps. Do them in order, because each step opens the next:

1. **Connect your AI.** On your Home, press the button for ChatGPT, Claude or Grok. Your AI gets its memory from here and can take care of things for you.
2. **Tell us about yourself.** Answer a few questions about your work in the [chat](/v1/chat). The answers become your profile.
3. **Give an agent its first job.** In the chat or on your Home, say in one sentence what you want done, for example "Gather the industry news every weekday morning and write a short summary." AIMEAT makes an agent from it, and you see it before it starts.
4. **Make your first app.** Ask for an app in the chat, and you get a link to the finished app.
<!--/store-->
<!--nostore-->
Start with one sentence. Open your [Home](/v1/home), your own page here, and write in the field what you want done, for example:

> Gather the industry news every weekday morning and write a short summary.

AIMEAT makes an agent from it. An agent is an AI that does one job for you, also while you are away. Making it takes a moment, because the AI first writes the agent's instructions. You see the agent on your Home before it starts, and you decide whether it starts.

You can also talk with the AI directly in the [chat](/v1/chat).

If you already use ChatGPT, Claude or Grok, connect it here, and it becomes your right hand: it sees the same information and does the same things as the chat. The buttons to connect are on your Home.
<!--/nostore-->

(The connection uses the open MCP standard (Model Context Protocol), which ChatGPT, Claude and Grok support. Want to know more? Read [modelcontextprotocol.io](https://modelcontextprotocol.io) or ask your AI: "What does MCP mean and how does it work?")

## AI credit and what this costs {#cost}

<!--store-->
Your purchase includes AI credit. AI credit is an amount in euros that pays for the AI's work here. You see how much is left at the top of your Home, in the chat and on the [AI credit page](/v1/profile?tab=ai).

The credit is used according to which model you use and how much – different models use it at different rates. You can buy more at any time: press Buy more AI next to the credit on your Home. It is a one-off payment, and no new subscription is needed.

When the credit is used up, you get a notice on your Home and in the chat. You can then buy more AI or start using an AI account of your own.

The monthly fee and the invoices are in the store. You find them in Settings, under Orders and invoices.
<!--/store-->
<!--nostore-->
The operator of this server decides what things cost here. If using the AI uses up a credit, you see what is left in the [AI settings](/v1/profile?tab=ai) and in the chat.

When the credit is used up, AIMEAT tells you and shows how to go on, for example with your own AI key (see Advanced below).
<!--/nostore-->

## Who sees my things {#privacy}

Everything you save is private at first. Only you see it.

You decide what you share. You can invite people to a shared place and share its workspaces with them. You can also publish something for everyone. What you share is seen only by those you shared it with.

An agent or a connected AI may do only what you allow it. You see your agents and connected AIs on the [Agents page](/v1/profile?tab=agents), and there you can change or take back their access. App permissions and access tokens are on the [Access and security page](/v1/profile?tab=access).

You can export all your data and delete your account in Settings.

## Agents and schedules {#agents}

An agent is an AI that does one job for you, also when you are away.

Say in one sentence in the [chat](/v1/chat) or on your [Home](/v1/home) what you want. AIMEAT makes the agent and shows it to you before it starts. If the work repeats, say so: for example "every weekday at 8 in the morning". Then the agent does the work on that schedule.

You approve the agent once. After that its runs show in the [Scheduler](/v1/profile?tab=scheduler), the list of when each agent works next. You see the finished work on your Home, under What has happened. If you want, the agent also sends it to your email. You can stop an agent at any time.

## AIs and agents from outside {#outside}

You can bring AIs and agents from elsewhere into your AIMEAT. They use the same memory, the same agents and the same apps as you. The more AIs work with you, the more your AIMEAT gets done.

**An AI you already use.** Claude, ChatGPT, Grok and Cursor connect over MCP. MCP (Model Context Protocol) is an open standard that lets an AI connect to other services. Read more at [modelcontextprotocol.io](https://modelcontextprotocol.io). Open [Connect your AI](/v1/profile?tab=mcp) and pick your AI, and the page shows the steps. At the end your AIMEAT asks what the AI may do, and you approve the connection.

**An agent that runs elsewhere**, for example a Grok bot or a CrewAI agent. The instructions are on the [Agents page](/v1/profile?tab=agents), under Connect the AI you already use. The agent asks for access, and the request shows on the same page under Pending Agent Requests. You approve it.

When you approve, you choose what the AI or agent may do: Read only, Standard, Full access or Custom. With full access it can do almost everything you can in your AIMEAT: write to memory, make agents and apps, and do work for you. Your account's security, secrets, payments, sharing and changes to permissions stay with you, unless you allow them one by one under Custom.

You can change or take back access at any time, and the change takes effect at once:

- You disconnect a connected AI on the [Connect your AI page](/v1/profile?tab=mcp) with the Disconnect button.
- You change an agent's access on the [Agents page](/v1/profile?tab=agents): open the agent and change its access, or press Delete agent. An AI you connected shows on the Agents page as a row of its own.
<!--/store-->

## Apps {#apps}

An app is a small web page that does one thing, for example a calculator, a form or a game.

Ask for an app in the [chat](/v1/chat), for example: "Make me an app to follow my customers' orders." You get a link to the finished app. Open it and use it: it has its own address, and its data is saved here. Your apps are also on the [Apps page](/v1/profile?tab=apps).

## Signing in {#signin}

You sign in with a sign-in link. Write your address in the sign-in window, and the link comes to your email. The link works once and for 15 minutes, and you need no password.

<!--store-->
The demo, the store and your own AIMEAT are three separate accounts. If something happens in one, it does not affect the others.

In the store ([store.aimeat.io](https://store.aimeat.io)) you find your invoices and your order. In your own AIMEAT you do your work. The demo ([aimeat.io](https://aimeat.io)) is a page open to everyone, to get to know AIMEAT.

![The demo, the store and your own AIMEAT are three separate accounts](/help/img/three-accounts-en.png)
<!--/store-->

<!--nostore-->
If you want, you can also sign in with your fingerprint, your face or your device's screen lock (a passkey). You turn it on on the [Access and security page](/v1/profile?tab=access).
<!--/nostore-->

## If something breaks {#broken}

Most things are safe to try. Everything is private at first, and an agent does only what you allow it.

If something does not work, write to [support@aimeat.io](mailto:support@aimeat.io). Say what you tried to do and what happened. If your AI is connected here, it can send the message for you.
