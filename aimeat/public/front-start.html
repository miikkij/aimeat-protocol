The same path in research

McKinsey: the value of AI grows in four levels

McKinsey studied 471 companies owned by private equity, across 31 industries. It sorted the value AI brings into four levels. The multiple is the company's value over its revenue, as a median.

McKinsey level 113x
Experiments Separate trials and personal tools. AI is not part of the workflows. AIMEAT levels 1–2
McKinsey level 214x
Efficiency AI is in the workflows. Productivity and margin improve. AIMEAT levels 3–4
McKinsey level 320x
The product changes AI is part of the product or the service. It starts to show in sales. AIMEAT level 5 opens this
McKinsey level 431x
New business The company builds new AI services and data products. AIMEAT level 5 opens this

Productivity alone does not raise value

Levels 1 and 2 are close: 13x and 14x. Value rises when AI changes what the company sells. AIMEAT levels 1 to 4 are the base. Level 5 opens McKinsey levels 3 and 4.

Our view: you do not need a separate AI team

McKinsey names the shortage of skilled people as the biggest obstacle, and says level 4 needs separate AI teams. When people and agents work on the same platform, AI is inside every team. You can build new services with the people you already have.

What the platform must give at each step

  1. 0 → 1Needed: the data stays with the company, and the tools are approved.AIMEAT gives: an AIMEAT of your own, in the company's own environment. The company picks its AI provider and uses its own key.
  2. 1 → 2Needed: shared knowledge and shared instructions.AIMEAT gives: shared workspaces where people collect decisions, instructions and ways of working that work. Ready-made app templates.
  3. 2 → 3Needed: the AI reads the company's data directly.AIMEAT gives: any AI connects with one connection (MCP). It reads and writes with the user's permissions.
  4. 3 → 4Needed: agents act on their own in a defined area.AIMEAT gives: every agent has its own identity and its own permissions. You decide whether an agent acts on its own, writes a draft or asks first. Work can start on a schedule or from an event.
  5. 4 → 5Needed: trust that rests on traceability.AIMEAT gives: every piece of content shows whether a person or an AI made it, with which model and from which sources. Decisions are recorded, and an agent's work stays visible afterwards.

Source: McKinsey, Beyond productivity: How AI creates value in private equity. Matching the levels to AIMEAT's levels is our own reading.

Agents can even run on your own computer

An agent is an AI that does the work on its own. You can run it on your own computer, and it works with your data. You decide what it may do.

  • You approve every agent before it starts work.
  • You choose what it may read and write.
  • You see every action, and every action is labelled.
  • You stop or remove an agent at any time.
