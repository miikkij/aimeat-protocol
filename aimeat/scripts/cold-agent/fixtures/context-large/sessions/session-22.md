None of these is a plan. I'll write to Hanna to tell her the news, mostly because she pushed us to apply and deserves to hear it from us first. Rasmus said we should also put the grant in the pilot summary at the end of June. We agreed to keep the announcement low-key until we know what we're doing with it.

## Moving the drop day

This was the main subject, and it took most of the morning.

**Background.** On 24 February we decided the dawn run would be on Tuesdays. That made sense then. We had one grower, one pod on the way and a first drop to aim at on 10 March, which was a Tuesday. Aino packed her own boxes on Mondays back then. A Tuesday drop gave her a day to pack and us a quiet early-week van day. Nobody was harvesting at the weekend for us, because there was hardly anyone to harvest for.

**What changed.** The situation is different now that all packing happens here in the Garage and there are five growers. Everything has to be at the Garage by Monday afternoon so I can run the pack line on Monday evening. In practice that means every grower picks over the weekend. I've heard about it from nearly all of them in the last few weeks, some more politely than others:

- Kaisa has been out on Sunday evenings with a headlamp.
- Juho said he hasn't had a free Saturday since he signed his GSA.
- Pekka put it most simply on the phone. He'd rather his greens went into the cabinet one day after picking, not two or three. He said the leafy things in particular don't enjoy sitting in his shed from Saturday to Tuesday morning.

That is the second problem, and to me the bigger one. The weekend harvest means the vegetables are older than they need to be when members open their compartments. It shows most in the greens, and it shows most at the end of the 48-hour window.

Rasmus asked, fairly, whether we were just moving the pain around. Would growers simply complain about a midweek harvest instead? We went through each grower's week as far as I know it, without asking them for anything new. None of them sell at a weekend market that would be affected. Liisa has told me more than once that midweek is easier for her than the weekend. I've only had the phone conversations, not a growers' circle, so I'll check with each of them before we switch. Still, everyone I've talked to has asked for some version of this.

**Decision: the dawn run moves to Thursdays, so growers harvest on Wednesday instead of at the weekend.** This replaces the Tuesday decision of 24 February. The reasoning in one line: Tuesday suited one grower packing her own boxes, and Thursday suits five growers feeding a pack line in the Garage.

What follows from it, as far as we could see this morning:

- **Pack line.** It moves to Wednesday evening, the evening before the dawn run, as before. Growers bring produce in on Wednesday afternoon. I'm glad my evenings shift around: Monday has been a long day with the sweep in the morning and packing until late.
- **Van day.** It becomes Thursday. I need to rebook the van with the hire place. Rasmus wondered whether Thursday will be busier for them than Tuesday was. I'll ask.
- **Box sheets.** Growers send their box sheet earlier in the week to match the Wednesday harvest. I'll agree the exact timing with each of them rather than set it from here.
- **Loop sheets.** They will be reprinted. The order of stops doesn't change, only the day at the top. Rasmus said he'd change the template so the day prints in larger letters, because I once took last week's sheet out with me.
- **Picker.** The drop day shows in several places: the box screen, the reminders, and the card on file charge, which follows the drop day. Rasmus will go through them all. Nothing about how members pay changes. They're simply charged on Thursday instead of Tuesday.
- **Members.** Each building gets a member note before the first Thursday drop, and building champions get a heads-up a little earlier so they aren't caught out by neighbours asking. I'll draft one text and Rasmus will push it per building.
- **The sweep.** It stays on Mondays for now. Rasmus pointed out that a Monday sweep is now three days before the drop instead of the day before, so it's less of a "get the pod ready" round. We didn't change anything and put "does the sweep want to move with the drop day?" on the parking lot.

One worry I raised and we didn't solve: the orphan run. With a Thursday drop and the window as it is, the window closes at the weekend, and the Harbour Pantry takes nothing at weekends. With Tuesday drops that never came up. I'll talk to Hilda before the switch. I want to understand how she'd like this handled, and I don't want to turn up with a fixed plan. Rasmus added it to the red list so it doesn't slip.

We also laughed that the Tuesday sit-down is now on a day with no drop in it at all. Rasmus says the name stays. The sit-down was never about the drop. It was about him having nothing in his calendar on Tuesday mornings.

We didn't fix the date of the first Thursday drop. That depends on every grower confirming and the van being rebooked. I'd rather do it right than do it next week.

## Dawn run timing and the seven o'clock line

Moving the day raised a related question about time of day, which we've been loose about.

We've always aimed to have every pod filled before the early leavers come down. We never wrote it down as a rule, and the timing has been fine until now. While we were going through the Thursday plan, I kept saying "as long as we're over the seven o'clock line". Rasmus asked what that was, and I realised I'd been saying it in my head for weeks. **The seven o'clock line** is the rule that every pod is filled before 7:00 on a drop day. If someone on an early shift comes down at seven, their box should already be there. Rasmus liked it because it's a line you either cross or don't, with no arguing about "roughly". He wrote it on the whiteboard, and it seems to have stuck.

The problem is that we're at six pods now, and the last stop has been getting close. Last week I was at Weaver's Yard later than I'd like. Part of that was me losing time at the front door of one building while waiting for the caretaker's fob to work. Most of it, though, is simply more stops on the same loop. Rasmus went through last week's loop sheet with me, stop by stop. With Ferry Close and Weaver's Yard on the end, there isn't much slack.

**Open: can the dawn run finish before 7:00 with six pods?** We didn't answer it. We need a few more runs, ideally on Thursdays and timed properly, before either of us will say yes or no. Rasmus wants the Field drop scans to give us a time for every stop so we can see where the minutes go, instead of me guessing from memory. That information is already in there. He just needs to pull it out.

Rasmus then floated something he'd clearly been thinking about: a **dusk run**. It would be an evening round on the day before, as the opposite of the dawn run (hence the name, which he was pleased with). The pods would be filled the night before, so the seven o'clock line stops being a race. We tried it out on paper with this week's loop sheet:

- **For it:** no early start, lobbies are open and lit, and caretakers are around if a hatch sticks. Boxes would be in place for anyone leaving at five in the morning.
- **Against it:** the dusk run would fall on the same evening as the pack line, so either one of us drives while the other packs alone, or the packing moves earlier. Boxes would also sit in dry cells overnight, which matters more in summer than now. On top of that, the 48-hour window would effectively start the evening before.
- **The thing that made me hesitate most:** lobbies are busy in the evening. Back-loading with people walking past and asking what's in the boxes is slower than at five in the morning, and every stop would take longer.

Neither of us argued for it strongly. We left the dusk run on the parking lot, next to the open question about the seven o'clock line, since if the answer to that one is no, the dusk run is one way out. Rasmus slipped and said "the K-12 hatches are easier to load at night", then corrected himself. They're all K-16s now and it doesn't matter. He just wanted it noted that he's still getting used to the name.

## Backroom

The last subject was short. Maija will be using the admin back end, and when I showed it to her last week her first question was whether she needed to know bookkeeping. She'd assumed "the Ledger" was an accounting tool. To be fair to her, that's what the word means. We named it in February when most of what it did was payments, and it has since grown into members, boxes, drops and payments all together.

Rasmus had a list of names. I vetoed "Admin" as too dull and "Control Room" as too dramatic. We settled on his last suggestion, **Backroom**: it's the room behind the shop, where the boxes and the lists live and members never go, which is exactly what it is.

**Decision: the Ledger is renamed Backroom, because Maija will use it and "ledger" sounded like accounting.** Rasmus will change the name where people see it: the login page, the header and the bookmarks. He laughed that the code still says Larder in half the places anyway, so one more old name underneath won't hurt anyone. I'll try to stop saying "the Ledger" in my own notes from today, though I give it about a week before I slip.
