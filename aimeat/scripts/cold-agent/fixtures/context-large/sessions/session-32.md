# Larder · session 32 · 18 August 2026 · Growers pack again

Hot again. By nine the Garage was already stuffy, so we pulled the door up, pointed the fan at the pack line tables and sat in the yard with the coffee. That felt like the right place for this conversation. Maija came in straight from a call with Liisa and had her laptop open before I'd poured anything.

There was really one subject today, and I had put it on the agenda myself last Tuesday: the pack line, and whether it should still be mine.

## Where we started from

On 12 May we decided that all packing moves to the Garage and that I run a pack line here on the evening before the dawn run. I want to write down why, because I'm the one undoing it and I don't want the note to read as if May was a mistake.

In the spring the boxes looked different from one grower to the next. Some came in crates, some in whatever the farm had lying about. Short boxes arrived at the pod with no swap card, because nobody at the farm could know that a member at Quarry Street 12 would open the door and see the gap. Packing everything in one room, in loop order, from one pack sheet, fixed most of that within a couple of weeks. That is what the standard box going up to 29 € was meant to pay for. At the midsummer review we counted the pack line as one of the things that had clearly worked. I still think it did, for the size we were then.

Since then, everything around the pack line has changed:

- The dawn run moved to Thursdays, so the growers harvest on Wednesday. The produce reaches the Garage on Wednesday afternoon, or early evening when someone's van is late. The pack line runs on Wednesday night, and the van leaves for the seven o'clock line a few hours later. Harvest, packing and loading all land inside the same eighteen hours.
- We have nine pods now, not four. Rasmus pulled the timestamps of the last tick on each pack sheet out of Backroom. Since July, far too many of my Wednesdays ended well after midnight. I knew that already, but it was worse seeing it as a column.
- Every vegetable is handled twice. The grower picks it, crates it, drives it, and then I uncrate it and repack it. Ville said weeks ago that his salad leaves don't like the second journey, and I've seen what he meant on the tables.
- The growers lose their Wednesday afternoon in the car. At the last growers' circle Pekka said he spends more of his harvest day on the coast road than in his beds. Others nodded, and at the time I took it as grumbling.
- In August the Garage is warmer than any farm's cold store. Produce that waits here between arrival and packing is waiting in the worst place we have.

The problem we fixed in May has also mostly gone away. The growers now work from their box sheets in Field every week. They know what a standard box should look like, because they have seen ours every Thursday since May. They know about swap cards. The consistency we needed the Garage for is now in their hands.

## The discussion

Rasmus went first, and his opening was a way to keep the pack line: split it over two evenings, or get me help on Wednesdays. We dropped the two evenings quickly, because there is nothing to pack on Tuesday when the harvest is on Wednesday. Help was a real option. Maija has the hours, in principle. I said that would be paying, by the hour, to keep a problem we could simply remove. Maija agreed, and said she'd rather spend her hours on the grower desk, where she is behind anyway.

Maija then argued for sending all of it back to the farms, the family boxes too, and doing no packing in the Garage at all. Her case was that "the Garage packs" and "the growers pack" is a simpler story to tell seven growers than a split. It is simpler. But I wanted to keep the family boxes here, for these reasons:

- There are few of them. Packing them is an hour on Wednesday evening, not a night.
- They are the boxes the growers have found the fiddliest. They are big and heavy, and they need the most careful balancing so the bottom layer survives the van.
- In my experience they carry the most add-ons, and the add-on shelf is here.
- I want my hands on some boxes every week. If I stop packing completely, I stop seeing what the produce actually looks like before it reaches a lobby. Kaisa's courgettes in July were the last time that saved us a complaint.
- The Garage is a registered food premises. I don't want to keep that registration up for a room we don't use.

Rasmus sided with me on the split, mostly for the last two reasons. Maija said she could live with it as long as the growers hear it from her as one clear sentence, not a diagram.

## The words

The two names came out of the morning almost by accident. On the phone with Liisa before she arrived, Maija had answered a question with "it would be grower-packed, like before". Liisa liked that and repeated it back to her. Maija used it twice more at the table without noticing. Rasmus then said he needed a name for the other kind too, because Backroom will have to mark every box as one or the other. He picked "hub-packed", since he has called the Garage the hub ever since packing moved here. The pair stuck at once because it is exactly the field Rasmus has to build. A grower-packed box is packed at the farm by its grower. A hub-packed box is packed here on the pack line.

## Decision

**Growers pack the standard and small boxes at the farm again (grower-packed); the Garage packs only the family boxes (hub-packed).** This replaces the decision of 12 May 2026, under which all packing moved to the Garage with a pack line here on the evening before the dawn run.

The reasons, in short:

- the Thursday dawn run puts harvest and packing on the same day;
- nine pods are too many boxes for one evening at the Garage;
- produce gets handled twice and loses its freshness in a warm room;
- the growers drive in on harvest day;
- the consistency problem from the spring is now solved at the farms.

We did not set a start week. Maija will speak to each of the seven growers first. Grower-packed starts with each grower when that grower says they are ready, and the dawn run picks up their crates from the farm instead of from Pier Road. Until then, a grower carries on delivering to the Garage as now. Rasmus was fine with a mixed week or two, as long as Backroom knows which box is which.

## What it touches

We went through the knock-on effects one by one. Most are small. Two are not settled, and I'm noting them as open, not decided.

**Pack sheet and loop sheet.** The pack sheet will only list hub-packed boxes, so on Wednesday evenings it will be short. The loop sheet needs to show where each crate comes from, farm or Garage. Otherwise whoever drives will be hunting through the van at the first stop. Rasmus thinks this is an afternoon's work once the flag exists in Backroom.

**Swap cards.** Growers need to print swap cards again at the farm. Rasmus thinks Field can already produce them from the box sheet, because the code is the same one the pack line uses. He wants to check that it prints properly from a phone, since most of the growers don't have a desk printer. Helmi certainly doesn't.

**Add-ons.** This is the real loose end. Add-ons live on the add-on shelf here. If a standard box is packed at Mossy Gate, the eggs don't travel with it. We talked about three ways round it, with no conclusion:

- growers take the add-ons out to the farm with their crates;
- the dawn run carries a separate add-on bag and puts it in at the stop;
- add-ons go only with hub-packed boxes. Rasmus floated this and then took it back himself, because it would quietly turn add-ons into a family box feature.

It goes on the red list for this week. Rasmus and Maija will come back with a proposal by next Tuesday.

**Prices.** Rasmus pointed out, fairly, that the standard box went to 29 € to pay for packing in the Garage, and most of that packing is now leaving. We didn't touch any price today and didn't want to rush it. Growers may also ask whether packing at the farm again changes their grower share. Maija will log the question if it comes up and answer that nothing has changed as of today.

**Rainy-day rule.** Nothing changes here. If a grower cancels a drop because of weather, the boxes are skipped and the make-good stands as before. It actually gets simpler: a cancelled grower-packed box never comes to the Garage at all, so I won't have half-filled crates sitting on the tables on a wet Wednesday.

**Pickup window.** Unchanged at 36 hours. Nothing about where a box is packed reaches the lobby end. Rasmus checked whether grower-packed crates loaded at the farm make the van any later for the seven o'clock line. The outer loop passes most of the farms anyway, so he thinks the loading time is a wash. He'll watch the first mixed week on the Pulse ("pod health", he said first, and then corrected himself, to Maija's great satisfaction).

**Crates.** Crates now have to get back to farms rather than to Pier Road. The crate deposit works exactly as before from the member's side. Behind the scenes, the van will bring empties out on the dawn run. Maija will ask each grower how many crates they want to keep at the farm, so we're not running one-crate shuttles.

**Onboarding pack.** The packing section currently says "deliver to the Garage by Wednesday afternoon" and not much more. Maija will rewrite it. She also wants a one-page "what a good standard box looks like" sheet with photos from our own pack line, so the spring problem doesn't creep back. Good idea. I'll pull the photos.

## Other bits

Mid-morning Rasmus took a call from Kestrel about the plinth at Ropewalk 3. Their fitter isn't happy with how it sits on that lobby floor and wants to come back with shims. Nothing for us to decide. Rasmus will find a time that suits the caretaker there.

Maija asked, half-joking, whether the empty Garage on Wednesday nights means I'll finally clear the corner with the old shroud panels. Possibly. I've put "what else the Garage is for" on the parking lot instead of answering. Rasmus immediately said "the swap market warehouse" and was ignored.

I'm a bit sad about the pack line, if I'm honest. It was the first thing in Larder that ran exactly the way I'd drawn it. But I'm not sad about my Wednesday nights. Maija said that makes it the right decision, and then went to call Aino before Aino heard about it from someone else.
