# Larder · session 11 · 24 March 2026 · Housing associations and champions

We held this one at the Garage, straight after the morning's run, so I'm writing it up with cold hands and a second coffee. Ilona had asked Sirpa, the chair of the Alder Court HA, to join us for the first half. Sirpa stayed for nearly the whole session. That was useful, and it changed the shape of the agenda.

Some context for why Sirpa came. Since pod zero went in, the Alder Court board has had a few questions from residents who aren't members. Most of them are the obvious ones: what is that thing in the lobby, who said it could stand there, and who pays for the electricity. Sirpa wanted to be able to answer those at their next board meeting without having to ring Ilona every time. We wanted to hear what the HA side actually worries about, rather than guess at it from the lobby walks. So we put the parts of the red list that touch HAs at the top and left the grower items for the end.

Weather note for the record: sleet on the van day, a wet floor mat at Alder Court, and the hatch seal looked fine. Ilona called it the morning loop twice this morning out of habit. Sirpa now thinks that's the official name, and I didn't correct her.

## Lobby fee

Sirpa opened with money, which I respect. Her point was simple. The board cannot stand up at the annual meeting and say they gave part of the common space to a business for nothing. Even if nobody minds the pod, someone will ask what the building gets, and "nicer vegetables for some of you" is not an answer the board can minute. She kept calling it "the rent".

Ilona didn't like "rent". It sounds like a lease, and a lease sounds like a contract with a notice period and lawyers. She said, "It's not rent, it's a lobby fee. We're paying for the lobby slot, not renting the lobby." It stuck at once, partly because it sits so neatly next to lobby slot and partly because Sirpa started using it herself within about two minutes. So: the lobby fee is what Larder pays an HA for a lobby slot.

Then we talked about how to set it.

- **A share of each box picked up in that building.** I floated this first. It sounded fair, but it ties the HA's income to our north number in a way that's odd for them, and it's fiddly to report. Sirpa said her treasurer would hate a number that changes every month.
- **Nothing in cash, but a free box for the board each week.** Ilona shot this down. It helps the five people on the board, not the building, and it looks worse at a meeting, not better.
- **A flat monthly sum per pod.** This is the one Sirpa wanted. It's easy to put in the budget, easy to explain, and it covers the socket question without anyone metering anything.

Electricity was the real sub-question. The chill cells run off the lobby's ordinary socket, and we're not changing that. Sirpa's view is that the fee is the building's answer when someone asks who pays for the electricity: Larder does, through the lobby fee. She doesn't want a separate electricity line in the agreement, and neither do we.

**Decision: Larder pays each HA a lobby fee of 40 € a month per pod.**

The reasons as we agreed them:

- It's a small, round, flat sum an HA board can minute and defend.
- It answers the electricity question without metering.
- Doing it per pod keeps it simple if a building ever has more than one.
- At this size it doesn't move the kill criteria or the pilot's sums in any way we lose sleep over.

Ilona will put the lobby fee into the short letter she sends after a lobby walk, so new HAs hear about it early and not as a surprise. Sirpa asked whether it would be backdated for Alder Court. Ilona said we'd sort that out with her directly. We didn't settle a date for it.

## Building champions

This is the part I think matters most from today, and it came out of Sirpa being blunt. Since the first drop, two Alder Court members have knocked on her door. One had a question about a wake code. The other wanted to know why the box had kale in it again. She's happy to help, but she is not our help desk. More to the point, she said she shouldn't be: if the HA chair becomes the person you complain to about Larder, the pod becomes the board's problem, and boards remove problems.

She said, more or less: "You need a champion in every building. Someone who actually gets a box, who people see at the pod on drop day, and who knows who to ring." Ilona wrote "building champion" on the whiteboard. By the end of the hour I'd shortened it to BC in my notes and in the new field I'm adding to the building record in the Ledger, and Ilona has started saying BC too. So: a building champion (BC) is a resident volunteer who speaks for Larder in one pod building.

What a BC does, as we talked it through. None of this is final, just the shape:

- Is the face of Larder in the lobby: answers neighbours' questions and points them at Picker.
- Tells us early when something in the lobby is off: the pod screen is dark, the honesty shelf looks picked over, a compartment door sticks.
- Is the person the HA can talk to, instead of the HA calling Ilona about every small thing.
- Is not responsible for orphan boxes. Those stay with the caretaker, as now. Sirpa was firm that she didn't want the BC to blur the caretaker's role.

That last point brought us back to the open question of whether caretakers should get something for helping with the pods. Sirpa had views (Raimo does more than his contract says, in her opinion). We didn't settle it today, and it's still open. I'm noting it so nobody thinks the champion discussion answered it. It didn't.

One champion or two per building? I wondered whether bigger buildings need two, with one per stairwell. Ilona and Sirpa both said one. Two people means each assumes the other one has it, and the HA doesn't know whose number to give out. One name, one phone number.

**Decision: each pod building has one building champion.**

We already have one, in effect. Oskar at Harbour Row, the resident there and not Oskar at Kestrel, told Ilona on her last visit that he'd happily "be the Larder guy" for the building. So he is Harbour Row's BC. Ilona will tell him it now has a name. I'll be careful in the Ledger to keep him apart from Kestrel's Oskar. I've already nearly sent a pod question to the wrong one once this month.

For Alder Court, Sirpa doesn't want to be chair and champion at once, and she's right. She'll ask around among members she knows. We won't name anyone until someone says yes.

## Champion perk

Next question: what does a BC get? Ilona called it the champion perk, half as a joke ("so what's the champion perk, a sash?"). The name stayed. It's what a building champion gets in return.

Options on the table:

- **Nothing; it's volunteering.** Sirpa said that works for about a month. After that people feel used, especially when a neighbour shouts at them about a short box.
- **A free box every week.** Generous, but it changes the relationship. It also starts to look like a job, and we didn't want to drift into paying someone through the back door.
- **Cash.** No. Same reason, and more paperwork.
- **A discount on their own boxes.** This keeps the champion a member like everyone else, scales with how much they actually use Larder, and is easy to do in Picker. I can set it as a per-member adjustment in the Ledger without new screens.

We picked the discount.

**Decision: a building champion gets 10 % off their boxes.**

Reasons: it's clearly a thank-you, not a wage. It only applies while they're a member, which is exactly the kind of person we want speaking for us. And it's simple to apply against the card on file without touching the grower share logic.

We noted that on a single box it eats most of our house cut. That's fine, because there will be one champion per building, but it's the number to keep an eye on. Ilona asked whether it should apply to a season pass too. We didn't decide that, so I'll treat it as "their boxes" for now and flag it if a champion buys one.

## Member notes

This came out of the champion discussion. Champions will want to tell their whole building things: the honesty shelf is restocked, drop is a bit late because of the sleet, someone left a bag in a compartment. Right now the only way is for a member to ask a neighbour, or for us to text people one by one, which I'm not doing.

I said the Ledger should be able to push a short message to all members of one building and nobody else. I was calling it a "building broadcast", which Ilona said sounded like a fire alarm. She said, "just call it a member note". So a member note is a short message pushed to all members of one building.

We didn't decide who can send one. Only us? Champions as well? Champions only after we approve the text? Sirpa's instinct is that the champion should be able to, because that's half the point. Mine is that anything that reaches a member's phone with "Larder" on it is ours to answer for. I'll build the plumbing in the Ledger so we can send them ourselves, and we'll come back to who presses the button.

## The member FAQ

Ilona had written this before the session and pasted it in for me to read, with Sirpa reading over my shoulder. It's the page every new member gets, so the questions people currently knock on Sirpa's door about are answered before the box ever arrives. Ilona called it the member FAQ, and that's what we're calling it. Here it is in full, as she wrote it:

---

> Larder member FAQ
>
> How does Larder work? Every week a grower near Lakeport packs your box, and on drop day we put it into your compartment in the pod in your lobby. You open the compartment with the wake code we send you by text message.
>
> How long can my box wait? You have 24 hours from the drop to collect it.
>
> What if I cannot collect it in time? An uncollected box is left with your building's caretaker, who keeps it for you.
>
> How do I pay? With a card on file in Picker. We charge it on the drop day.
>
> What does a box cost? A standard box is 24 €.
>
> What is the shelf on top of the Alder Court pod? Our honesty shelf: take surplus vegetables and pay by phone.
>
> Who can I talk to? Your building champion, or Larder by email.

---

I checked it line by line against what's in force: the 24 hours, the caretaker for uncollected boxes, the card on file charged on drop day, the 24 € standard box, and the honesty shelf at Alder Court only. It all matches.

I asked whether it should mention the season pass. Ilona wants it short, so it stays as it is, and the season pass can live in Picker where people buy it. Sirpa liked that the last line points to the building champion and not to the HA, which was rather the point of her coming.

**We both agree it goes to every new member as it is.** Existing members get it too. Ilona will send it with the next box rather than invent a special occasion.

I noticed the FAQ never says "customer" anywhere. Ilona's old word has fully gone from what she writes, though she still says it out loud.

Sirpa asked one technical question while reading. Could a door pulse ever open the wrong compartment, say if two members' codes got mixed? I explained that the wake code is tied to one compartment for one drop, and that the spine only opens what the Ledger names. She seemed satisfied. I've put "explain this in plain words somewhere" on the parking lot rather than in the FAQ.

## Insurance (raised, not settled)

Near the end, Sirpa asked the question I'd been half-avoiding: who insures a pod standing in a lobby? Her examples were concrete. A resident's child pulls the hatch and gets hurt. Someone kicks in the screen on the spine. The plinth marks the floor tiles. Or there's a water leak in the lobby, and the chill cells are part of the damage claim. Is that the HA's building policy, Kestrel's, because it's their pod on lease, or ours?

We don't know, and we didn't pretend to. It's open. Sirpa will look at what the HA's policy says about third-party equipment in common spaces. I'll ask Oskar at Kestrel what the lease says about damage and who they expect to insure the unit on site. Ilona will look at what Larder itself would need. Ilona wants it on the red list, because she doesn't want to do another lobby walk without at least an honest "we're finding out". I agreed it goes on.
