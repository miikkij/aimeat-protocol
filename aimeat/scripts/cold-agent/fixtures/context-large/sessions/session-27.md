# Larder · session 27 · 14 July 2026 · Back to Larder

We started late because Ilona had been at the Garage since six sorting crates, and the first half hour was coffee and complaining about the heat. The lobby at Mill Gate was apparently like a greenhouse on Thursday morning. Then we got to the thing we had both been circling for two weeks without saying out loud: the name.

## Cropbox, three months on

Quick recap for my own sake. On 14 April we renamed the product Cropbox. The reason was real at the time. On lobby walks, people kept hearing "Larder" and assuming we were a pantry-storage app, something to track what's in your cupboards. Ilona had at least three conversations that went sideways for five minutes before anyone mentioned vegetables. Cropbox said "vegetables in a box" on the tin, so we went with it. Picker got a new header, the shroud went green with the Cropbox mark, and Ilona started saying Cropbox on every lobby walk.

What we never managed was the web address. I tried more times than I want to admit, through every route I could think of, and the cropbox address was simply not for sale to us. We limped along on a longer workaround address that nobody could remember or spell back to us. Every time I had to read it out on the phone I could hear the other person writing down something wrong.

The bigger problem is that nobody actually switched. Ilona went through her phone during the session and it was almost funny:

- Aino at Ridgeback still says "Larder" in every message, including one last week about cabinet space for the Thursday drop.
- Juho and Kaisa both say "the Larder boxes" on the growers' WhatsApp. Ville wrote "Cropbox (Larder)" once, as if translating for himself.
- Oskar at Harbour Row, our building champion there, told Ilona the neighbours call it "the Larder thing in the lobby" and he has stopped correcting them.
- Members asking about make-goods or skip weeks say "my Larder box" far more often than "my Cropbox". Ilona counted roughly, and Cropbox was the minority. We didn't write the count down, and it doesn't matter.

There is also our own decision from session 22-ish, when the box tiers got names. We deliberately kept the standard tier as Larder "for the members who still say Larder." Looking back, that was us admitting the name had already won. And the Larder layer in the code never changed. I never even opened a ticket for it.

## The U-turn

So we talked about going back. Ilona was the one who said it first: "Fine, let's just do the U-turn and stop pretending." I said the U-turn sounds like a driving term and we'll confuse it with the loop. She said that was exactly why it would stick, because we'd remember it every time the van turns around at the end of Ropewalk. It stuck. By the end of the session we were both saying "after the U-turn" for anything that needs renaming back, so I'm writing it down: **the U-turn** is our name for going back from Cropbox to Larder.

We tried to be honest about the original problem, because we didn't rename for no reason in April. Does the pantry-app confusion go away? Not entirely, probably. But the confusion happened on lobby walks, before people had seen a pod. Once the pod is standing on its lobby slot with vegetables visible through the chill cell doors, nobody thinks it's a cupboard-inventory app. Ilona's view is that the first ninety seconds of a lobby walk are her job, not the name's job, and she'd rather fix it with a better opening line than carry a name nobody uses. I agreed. The other side of the scale is a name we can't get the address for and that our own growers and members won't say. That's not close.

We also checked whether going back costs us anything with the HAs. Every HA we've met since April heard Cropbox. Ilona thinks the chairs won't care. Sirpa at Alder Court never stopped saying Larder either. The wave 2 buildings are the only ones where people may have learned Cropbox first, and Ilona will mention it in passing next time she's there rather than make an announcement.

**Decision: the product goes back to the name Larder (the U-turn).** This replaces the 14 April decision to rename it Cropbox. Reasons: the cropbox web address could not be bought, and growers and members kept saying Larder anyway. The April reason (Larder mistaken for a pantry-storage app on lobby walks) is acknowledged. We think it's better handled on the walks themselves than by a name that didn't take.

Closing an open question while we're here: since 14 April we've had "Does the name Cropbox clash with an existing trademark?" sitting there. I never got further than a first search. **That question is now settled: it no longer matters, because the name went back to Larder.** Off the list. Ilona said that was the one good thing about the domain saga: it saved us a trademark lawyer.

I said we should at least do a quick check on Larder for the same reason, so we don't repeat the exercise. Ilona said "after the summer". I've put it on the parking lot, not the red list. It's a worry, not a decision.

## The shroud

This took longer than the name, which tells you something about us.

In April we decided the shroud would be green with the Cropbox mark. The green made sense next to "crop": leafy, fresh, obvious. Now that the mark is going, the shroud has to change anyway, so the question was whether to keep green and just swap the mark, or rethink the colour.

Ilona argued for ochre. Her points:

- Green with a Larder mark looks like a garden centre. Larder, as a word, is warmer and more indoor: a cupboard, a pantry, bread. A yellow-brown earthy colour fits it better.
- Most of our lobbies are grey or beige with green plants in the corner. Green pods disappear into that. At Quarry Street 12 there is literally a fake ficus next to the pod, and from the door you can't tell which is which.
- Ochre reads as food without shouting "salad".

My worry was dirt. Ochre shows scuffs, and the pods get bumped by prams and bikes. Ilona pointed out that the green ones show scuffs too, we just stopped seeing them, and the Monday sweep wipes the shroud anyway. I also wondered aloud whether a brighter shroud draws more attention to the pods and so more shrink. Neither of us has any data on that. Our shrink so far has been about boxes and crates, not people messing with the front of a pod. We left it as a thought and moved on.

We looked at a few ochre swatches on my laptop, which is a useless way to judge colour. Ilona wants to see a real panel in a real lobby before we print nine of them, which is fair.

**Decision: the shroud becomes ochre with the Larder mark.** This replaces the 14 April decision (green with the Cropbox mark). The Cropbox mark has to go after the U-turn, and green fits the old name more than the new-old one. Ochre suits Larder and stands out in the lobbies instead of blending into the plants.

Practical notes on the swap, all to-do, nothing decided:

- I need to find out from Oskar at Kestrel how the shroud comes off the K-16 and goes back on. I don't want us improvising with a screwdriver next to the spine. Last time someone opened the front panel at Alder Court, the frost guard setting got knocked into an odd state and I spent an evening reading heartbeats to work out why the chill cells were cycling. I'd like to know exactly which panels are safe to touch.
- The swap must not get in the way of back-load. Ideally it's done on a day with no drop, so nobody is at the hatch while someone else is at the front. Ilona wondered whether it fits into the Monday sweep. I think it's too much for one person on top of the sweep. Not decided. Ilona and Maija will look at it.
- We don't need all nine at once. A pod with a green shroud for a few more weeks hurts nobody.

## What else says Cropbox

I went through everything I own in software while Ilona refilled the coffee. The list was longer than I hoped:

- **Picker:** the header and the sign-in screen say Cropbox. The QR stub page has the Cropbox mark at the top. The wake code SMS says "Your Cropbox box is ready", which was always a clumsy sentence, so good riddance.
- **Field:** the drop scan confirmation screen says Cropbox. The growers never cared, but it should match. The box sheet screen says nothing either way.
- **Backroom:** internal. Maija sees Cropbox in a couple of page titles. Not urgent, but she'll ask.
- **Messages:** the make-good message template and the neighbour credit message both say Cropbox. The neighbour credit one matters most, because it goes to people who aren't members yet, so it's the first time they see the name.
- **The Larder layer:** nothing to do. It's the one thing that was right all along. Ilona said "the code knew."

I'll do Picker and the messages first, then Field, then Backroom. None of this is hard. It's mostly finding every string. I'll keep the Cropbox spellings searchable in the code for a while in case anything still points there.

Ilona asked whether we should keep the long workaround address running so old links don't break. Yes, obviously, for a while. We didn't discuss how long.
