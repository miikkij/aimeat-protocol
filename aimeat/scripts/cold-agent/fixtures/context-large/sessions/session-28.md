# Larder · session 28 · 21 July 2026 · Champions and credits

Hot morning, and the Garage was already stuffy by the time Rasmus arrived with the coffee, so we moved the sit-down onto the bench by the door with the fan on an extension cord. Maija joined from the start this time, because two of the three things on today's list touch Backroom and she is the one who lives in it most days now. The red list was short: one sticky door at Kiln Lane that Rasmus thinks is the lock controller, not the door, and the usual worry about ice bricks in the dry cells if this heat keeps up through Thursday. Neither needed us today.

The real agenda was the champions. Over the last few weeks a couple of things have piled up around the building champions and the perks that hang off them, and we agreed to deal with them in one go instead of patching each one.

## The champion perk

Back on 24 March we decided that a building champion gets 10 % off their boxes. At the time it made sense. We had wave 1 to fill, we were asking residents to stand in the lobby and answer questions for us, and a percentage felt fair and easy: the more boxes you take, the more you get back. It was also the first champion perk we could build without any new screens, because Backroom (still the Ledger then) already knew how to apply a discount to a card on file.

What has happened since is that nobody notices it. Maija has had more than one champion ask her, quite politely, whether they actually get anything, and when she showed them the discount on their drip plan charge the reaction was a shrug. Ten per cent of a 29 € box is under three euros. It disappears into the weekly charge. Rasmus put it well: it is a perk that only exists in the accounts. And it behaves oddly at the edges. A champion who takes a skip week gets nothing that week, which is exactly the week they might still be in the lobby helping a neighbour with a QR stub. A champion on the Harvest tier gets more than one on Sprout for doing the same job. None of that is wrong, exactly, but it isn't what we meant.

I also said, and Rasmus agreed, that anything which needs Backroom to recalculate every charge is a small tax on us forever. We have been here before with the SP refunds in the spring. Every exception we build into the money ends up costing Maija an afternoon at some point.

So we went through options. A flat credit each month (simple, but it is still just a number on a screen). A free add-on each week (nice, but the add-on shelf is already the messiest corner of the Garage and Maija pulled a face). Something physical, that shows up in their compartment and that they can point at.

Rasmus was the one who said "just give them a box". He was half joking, but it held up. A free small box each month is something the champion actually opens, it is the same for everyone regardless of tier, and it costs us something we can see and plan for on the pack sheet. While we were talking it through he kept calling it "the champion box" because he needed something to type into the Backroom line he was sketching, and by the third time Maija was saying it too. It stuck because it says exactly what it is.

**Decision: the champion perk becomes the champion box: one free small box a month, instead of the 10 % off.** This replaces what we decided on 24 March. The reasons, for the record: the discount was too small to notice, it varied with tier and skip weeks in ways we never intended, and it meant Backroom adjusting charges every week. The champion box is visible, the same for everyone, and it is one extra box on one pack line a month per building.

We talked about whether the champion box should come from a particular grower or rotate. Left it alone for now; Maija thinks the growers will have opinions and she would rather ask them than tell them. We also didn't settle which week of the month it goes in. Rasmus will look at what is easiest in Backroom and bring it back.

One more worry, which I want written down so I don't forget it: some champions will read this as us taking something away, because a percentage sounds bigger than it is. Maija suggested that when we tell them, we lead with the box and not with the maths. I agree.

## The neighbour credit

This one is less pleasant.

On 21 April we decided that a member who brings in a neighbour gets a 5 € neighbour credit, and so does the neighbour. The thinking then was that our best salespeople are members standing at the pod, and that a credit on both sides makes it easy to say yes. It worked, as far as we could tell. Sign-ups in some buildings came in pairs, and we liked seeing that.

Then Oskar called me last week. Our Oskar at Harbour Row, the building champion there, not Oskar at Kestrel; I'm writing it out because Rasmus's first question in the room was "the lock guy?". Harbour Row Oskar had noticed that members were signing up their own second phone numbers for the neighbour credit. A work phone, an old phone, a partner's number that is really the same household. The "neighbour" is the same person, or the same flat, and the 5 € goes out twice to one kitchen. He wasn't angry, he was mostly embarrassed for the building, and he said he only spotted it because he knows who lives where. That is exactly the kind of thing a champion is for, and exactly the kind of thing Backroom can't see.

Rasmus's first instinct was the obvious question: how do we catch it? We spent a good while on that.

- Match the card on file. Doesn't work: plenty of households have two cards, and plenty of real neighbours would never share one anyway.
- Match flat numbers. Backroom has the flat for most members, but not reliably, and two flats can be one family. It would also mean asking for flat numbers more strictly than we want to.
- Require the neighbour's first box to be picked up before either credit lands. Better, but someone using their own second number will happily pick up their own second box.
- Ask champions to vouch for new pairs. Maija said no to this immediately, and she's right. We'd be turning a volunteer into a ticket inspector.

Every route was either easy to get around or made the honest members do more work. Rasmus said it reminded him of the honesty shelf, and that got a groan from everyone. The lesson from that one was that a small amount of money left open will leak, and the effort of guarding it costs more than the money. About 40 % walked off the shelf back then. We don't know how big the leak is here and we decided we don't need to know to make the call.

**Decision: the neighbour credit is dropped.** Members were signing up their own second phone numbers, and we can't catch that without making sign-up worse for everyone. This replaces what we decided on 21 April. The question Rasmus raised today, how to catch abuse of the neighbour credit, is settled the simple way: we don't catch it, we stop offering it.

I want it noted that nobody is cross with members over this. A 5 € credit that you can claim by typing in your other number is an invitation, and we sent it. We didn't talk about going after any credits already given, and nobody wanted to. Rasmus will switch the credit off in Picker sign-up and keep the old records in Backroom as they are.

There was a half-idea about some other way to reward members who bring neighbours, something that can't be claimed by one person alone. Nothing concrete. It goes on the parking lot.

## Champions become lobby hosts

This came out of the neighbour credit conversation, oddly. When Maija was arguing against making champions vouch for sign-ups she said something like "they're not champions of anything, they're more like hosts, they stand in the lobby and make people feel welcome". Rasmus said "lobby host" back to her, partly because everything else we have is lobby-something already, and we all just started using it. By the end of the hour none of us were saying "champion" for the people any more, only for the box.

The longer reasoning, as we talked it through:

- "Champion" has always sounded a bit like a competition, or like a sports club. Maija says a few residents have been shy about taking the job for exactly that reason. They don't want to sound like they are selling something.
- What the job actually is, day to day, is being the friendly face by the pod. Showing someone how the QR stub works. Telling us when a door sticks. Noticing things, like Oskar did. "Host" describes that.
- BC as a shorthand was never great either.

**Decision: building champions are called lobby hosts from now on. The champion box keeps its name.**

We did argue a little about the second half of that. Rasmus wanted to rename the box too, for tidiness, but "lobby host box" is a mouthful and "host box" sounds like something from a hotel. Maija's point settled it: the box is what they get for championing the building, and the word still fits there even if it doesn't fit the person. Also, frankly, Rasmus had already typed "champion box" into Backroom an hour earlier, and nobody wanted to go through another rename this summer after the U-turn.

So: lobby host for the person, champion box for the box. I expect we'll slip for a few weeks. Rasmus was already teasing me for saying "morning loop" earlier in the session, so I'm in no position to police anyone.

Rasmus wants the change in Backroom labels and in Picker wherever members can see the word. Maija will go through the onboarding wording on her side so new lobby hosts never hear the old name from us.

## Telling people

None of this lands well if a lobby host hears it secondhand. We agreed the order is: lobby hosts first, personally, then members. I'll ring each lobby host this week. Oskar at Harbour Row first, partly to thank him and partly because he should hear what came of his call before anyone else. I'll tell each of them about the champion box and the new name in the same conversation, and lead with the box, as Maija suggested.

For members, the only change they will feel is the neighbour credit disappearing. Rasmus will draft a short member note, one per building, saying plainly that the credit has ended. We went back and forth on whether to explain why. Leaning towards a light touch: say it's ended, thank people who brought neighbours in, no finger-pointing. I'll read the draft before anything goes out.
