> ## Season pass: should Larder offer one, and how should it be priced?
>
> ### 1. What I was asked
> You asked whether Larder should let members pay for a run of weekly boxes up front (a "season pass", SP) during the pilot, and if so, what length and discount make sense. I've worked only from the figures you gave me. Anything marked *estimate* is my own assumption, not data.
>
> ### 2. Figures I'm working from
>
> | Item | Value |
> |---|---|
> | Standard box price | 24 € |
> | House cut | 15 % → 3.60 € per box |
> | Grower share | 85 % → 20.40 € per box |
> | Payment today | card on file in Picker, charged on the drop day |
> | Pickup window | 24 h from the drop |
> | Orphan boxes | left with the building's caretaker |
> | Pods in the pilot | 4 |
> | North number | boxes picked up per week, target 100 |
> | First drop | 11 boxes, Alder Court |
>
> The 15 % cut matters a lot here. At 3.60 € per box Larder's margin is thin, so almost any discount on a pass comes out of it unless someone else shares it.
>
> ### 3. What a pass could do for you
> 1. **Commitment, and so pickup.** A member who has paid for ten or twelve weeks has a reason to go down to the lobby every week. *Estimate:* prepaid members might collect a few percentage points more often than pay-per-drop members. You have one drop of history, so treat this as a hypothesis to test, not a benefit to count on.
> 2. **Fewer failed charges.** Weekly charges on the drop day can fail: expired cards, limits, bank checks. One charge per pass removes most of these, and with them the awkward question of whether a box goes into a compartment for someone whose payment didn't go through.
> 3. **Predictable volume for growers.** If Aino, Pekka and Liisa know that part of next month's boxes are already paid for, box sheets get easier to plan and planting decisions become a little less of a guess.
> 4. **Cash timing.** Money arrives before the boxes do. For a pilot with no outside money this helps, but it is also a liability (see 4.4), so I wouldn't make it the main reason.
> 5. **A cleaner signal on the bet.** Members who pre-commit to lobby pickup are stronger evidence for the bet than members who sign up week by week.
>
> ### 4. What it costs and risks
> 1. **The discount has to come from somewhere.** Under the current split every box carries a 20.40 € grower share. A pass box that brings in less than that is delivered at a loss.
> 2. **Prepaid orphan boxes.** Today an uncollected box has already been charged, and the conversation with the member is simple. With a pass, the member feels they "paid and missed it". You'll need a written rule on whether a box left with the caretaker counts as delivered.
> 3. **Pauses and holidays.** Members on a long pass will want to skip weeks. Every pause rule is more logic in the Ledger and Picker, and more support questions.
> 4. **Refunds if things change.** A pass promises future boxes. If the pilot pauses, ends or changes format, you owe members the unused weeks. The longer the pass, the bigger that tail.
> 5. **Grower shortfalls.** An incomplete prepaid box will feel worse to a member than an incomplete pay-per-drop box. You may want a policy for incomplete boxes before you sell any passes.
> 6. **Admin load.** The Ledger has to track a pass balance per member, reduce it on each drop and show it in Picker. A small job, but not nothing for one developer.
>
> ### 5. Pricing options
> Assuming the grower share stays at 20.40 € per delivered box:
>
> | Option | Boxes | Member pays | Per box | Discount | Larder per box | Larder per pass |
> |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
> | Pay per drop (reference) | 1 | 24.00 € | 24.00 € | – | 3.60 € | – |
> | 8 for the price of 7 | 8 | 168.00 € | 21.00 € | 12.5 % | 0.60 € | 4.80 € |
> | 10 for the price of 9 | 10 | 216.00 € | 21.60 € | 10 % | 1.20 € | 12.00 € |
> | 12 with 2 free | 12 | 240.00 € | 20.00 € | 16.7 % | −0.40 € | −4.80 € |
>
> I've included the 8-week option for completeness. It gives a bigger discount than 10-for-9 but builds less habit, so it gets the worst of both.
>
> With the grower share held constant, the 12-week option loses money on every box. That changes if the pass discount is shared with growers in proportion to the current split:
>
> | Option, discount shared pro rata | Larder per box | Grower per box |
> |---|---|---|
> | 10 for the price of 9 | 3.24 € | 18.36 € |
> | 12 with 2 free | 3.00 € | 17.00 € |
>
> On the 12-week pass, growers would then give up 3.40 € per box in return for guaranteed volume over the length of the pass.
>
> ### 6. Recommendation
> I'd offer a **12-week pass with 2 free boxes (240 € for 12)**, with the discount shared pro rata with growers through a short addendum to each GSA. My reasoning:
>
> 1. **Length builds habit.** Twelve weeks is long enough for lobby pickup to become routine. Ten is close; eight probably isn't.
> 2. **The message is simple.** "Two boxes free" is an easier pitch on a lobby walk than "10 % off", and 240 € is a round number.
> 3. **The discount is big enough to move people.** At 10 %, many members may not bother switching from pay-per-drop. At about 17 %, more will.
> 4. **Shared, it stays positive.** At 3.00 € per box Larder keeps most of its normal margin, and growers get a forward commitment they don't have today.
>
> Conditions I'd attach:
> - Cap the number of passes sold at first, so that one bad week or a wave of refunds can't swamp you.
> - Decide in writing whether a box left with the caretaker counts as delivered.
> - Allow a small number of skips per pass, so it doesn't turn into a support burden.
>
> If growers won't accept a lower share on pass boxes, the 12-week option doesn't work, and I'd fall back to a shorter pass with a smaller discount.
>
> ### 7. Things to settle before you decide
> 1. **How long is the pilot?** A 12-week pass sold in late March runs into June. If the pilot ends before a pass does, you owe boxes or refunds.
> 2. **Will growers take a lower share on pass boxes?** The house cut was only just lowered so that the grower share works for them. This needs a real conversation, not an assumption.
> 3. **What happens to an uncollected pass box?**
> 4. **What happens when a grower comes up short in a pass week?**
