# Larder · session 34 · 1 September 2026 · Two dawn runs a week

Rain all afternoon, the heavy late-summer kind that makes the Garage door stick halfway. Rasmus brought coffee from the kiosk on Pier Road. Maija came straight from a grower call with the grower desk binder under her arm, and it is getting thick. We had one real subject today, the drop day, and it took nearly the whole sit-down. I'm writing it up properly because we went back on a June decision and then changed our minds again within the same hour. I want the reasoning on paper before it blurs.

## Where we stand

A quick look at the numbers first, so the rest makes sense:

- 9 pods, all K-16, now that Kiln Lane, Saltworks and Ropewalk 3 are in. Wave 2 is complete.
- 131 boxes picked up last week. The north number target is 160 by 30 September, the end of the extension. We have four weeks and a gap of 29 boxes.
- Orphan rate still at 3 %.
- Churn was 9 % before the cut. It is too early to say whether 26 € brings it down. That question stays open, and we agreed not to read anything into a single week.

Rasmus had the Pulse up on the big screen. He wanted to show us what the chill cells actually do across a box week. He said "the Ledger" once while pulling it up, and Maija gave him a look.

## Why we were talking about the drop day at all

Two things have been nagging at me since the Harvest tier went to 39 €.

The first is the family box itself. We priced it so it is a clear saving against a standard box and a small box bought together. But a family box is a lot of vegetables to keep a household going for seven days. Nobody needs a survey to know what the leafy part of a Harvest box looks like by the following Tuesday. The roots are fine, the salad isn't. If the box is a saving on paper and half a bin of wilted greens in practice, it isn't a saving. I think that matters more for keeping Harvest members than the price does.

The second is what Rasmus showed on the Pulse. Each K-16 has four chill cells. On a Thursday-only drop they fill before seven, get picked over the next 36 hours, and then sit empty, cold and doing nothing until the next Thursday. Across nine pods that is a lot of refrigerated space we pay for and use for a day and a half a week. Rasmus put it bluntly: "The lease doesn't care how often we open the hatch." The pods cost the same per month whether we fill them once a week or twice. The same goes for every lobby slot we fought for.

Put together, that points at a second dawn run.

## Going back on the June decision

I want to be fair to the 9 June decision, because it was right when we made it. We moved the dawn run to Thursdays so that growers would harvest on Wednesday instead of at the weekend. That gave them their weekends back and gave members boxes picked the day before, not three or four days before. It also meant one van day, one pack line evening and one loop to plan. With wave 1 and the first two wave 2 buildings, that was the sensible shape. The growers liked it, and Aino said so more than once.

What changed is mainly scale and the family box:

- With nine pods, everything lands on one morning: the outer loop, the cargo bike leg and the seven o'clock line on every stop. One bad Thursday (a flat tyre, a sticky door at Ferry Close, a short box that needs a swap card written in the van) now spoils the week for every building at once.
- One drop a week means a family box has to last seven days, which is the problem above.
- One drop a week means the chill cells turn over once. We are four weeks from 30 September and need more boxes picked up, not more empty cold space.

Rasmus's worry, which I share, is cost. A second run means a second van day and a second cargo bike leg every week. The house cut only went to 14 % because the cargo bike leg and the sales share cost more than planned. We did not put a figure on it today. Rasmus thinks two lighter runs may not cost much more per drop than one heavy one, because the heavy Thursday loop already runs long, but that is a hunch until he works out the CPD. That's on his list.

Maija asked about the growers. A second run means picking twice a week, for some of them more of a change than for others. The floor price of 18 € per standard box doesn't change, and nobody is talking about touching the GSAs today. She will ring round all seven before anyone hears about it from a member.

I mentioned that at least nobody is on an SP any more. In the spring a change of drop day would have meant re-dating prepaid weeks and doing the refund maths all over again. On the drip plan the card on file is simply charged on whichever day the box drops. Rasmus said that is the first time he has been glad we dropped it.

## How "twin run" got its name

For the first twenty minutes we kept saying "two dawn runs a week", which is a mouthful. Rasmus tried "double run". I said that sounds like a cousin of the double drop, the last thing we want a driver to have in their head at six in the morning. Maija had been writing the two days on the whiteboard as two columns side by side, and she said "so it's a twin run". It stuck straight away. It's short, it doesn't sound like a mistake, and it describes what's on the board: two runs in the same week, the same shape, going round the same loop.

## First decision: Mondays and Thursdays

We decided:

- **There are two dawn runs a week, on Mondays and Thursdays: the twin run.** A family box no longer has to last a full week, and the chill cells empty twice a week instead of once. This replaces the 9 June decision that moved the dawn run to Thursdays.

The thinking behind Monday was simply to keep Thursday, which growers and members already know, and add a day roughly opposite it. Rasmus started sketching how Backroom would hold two drop days per pod. Maija started a list of which growers she would ring first.

## Changed minutes later: Tuesdays and Fridays

It lasted about ten minutes. Rasmus drove the loop last week, so he did the sweep last Monday, and he was the one who stopped. "Hang on. Monday is the sweep." The sweep is done by whoever drives the loop that week, and the whole point is that the pods are empty. You wipe the cells, check the doors, look at the plinth and the spine, and clear anything left in a dry cell. A Monday dawn run means arriving at each pod with fresh boxes and the sweep in the same morning. Either the sweep gets done around a pod full of boxes, which means no sweep at all for those cells, or it gets done before seven, which means a driver up at four. Neither is going to happen reliably.

We could have moved the sweep, but Monday is the one morning in the week when every pod is reliably empty, and that is why it's there. It made more sense to move the runs.

So we changed it:

- **The twin run is on Tuesdays and Fridays, not Mondays and Thursdays, because Monday is the day of the sweep.** This changes the decision of ten minutes earlier.

Tuesday and Friday works out better in a few other ways:

- A Tuesday drop's pickup window closes on Wednesday evening, so the chill cells are free well before Friday's drop. With 36 hours there is no risk of a chill lock carrying over.
- The sweep stays on Monday with every pod empty after the weekend.
- Maija pointed out, once we'd changed it, that Monday and Thursday would have meant growers picking on Sunday for the Monday run. That would have quietly undone half of what we decided in June. With Tuesday and Friday, a grower who keeps picking the day before picks on Monday and Thursday, both weekdays. That wasn't why we changed it, but it's a good reason to be glad we did.
- The pack line moves to the evening before each run for the Harvest boxes, since those are still hub-packed. Two pack line evenings instead of one, on Monday and Thursday.

The gaps are uneven: two days from Tuesday to Friday, three from Friday to Tuesday. Rasmus asked whether that matters for Harvest boxes. I don't think it does. The point is that no box has to last seven days.

## Things that came up and are not decided

**Friday orphans.** With a 36-hour window, a Friday drop's window closes on Saturday evening. The orphan run is weekdays only and the Harbour Pantry takes nothing at weekends, so any orphan box from a Friday drop sits until Monday. We didn't solve this today. It goes on the red list, because we can't start the twin run without an answer. With the orphan rate at 3 % it isn't many boxes, but it is chill cell space and it's food.

**How members are spread over the two days.** We did not decide whether members choose a day in Picker, whether whole buildings go on one day or the other, or how Harvest boxes ride the two runs. Rasmus sketched two ways of doing it in Backroom and wants a day to think. Maija wants to hear from the growers first, because some may only manage one picking day. Member choice of day in Picker went on the parking lot for now.

**Caretakers.** Every caretaker now gets two drop mornings a week instead of one. For most buildings the hatch side doesn't need them, but Raimo at Alder Court likes to know when the van is coming. I'd rather each caretaker hears it from me than finds a van outside on a Tuesday.

**Lobby hosts.** Each building needs a member note when we change the days. I'll draft one and run it past a couple of the lobby hosts first. Oskar at Harbour Row is always quick to tell us when something reads wrong to residents.

**The sit-down itself.** Rasmus noticed that our Tuesday sit-down now falls on a run day. Whoever drove the loop that morning will be half asleep by the afternoon. We left it for now. It isn't a decision anyone needs today, and we'll see how the first few Tuesdays feel.

**Parking lot.** Rasmus half-joked that if the twin run works we could try a dusk run on one of the two days. It went on the parking lot with "don't" written next to it in my handwriting.

**Start date.** We did not set one. The note can't go out until the growers are on board, the CPD is done and the Friday orphan question has an answer. I'd like to see at least a few twin run weeks before 30 September, but that's a wish and not a decision.

The rain stopped just as we finished, which Maija took as a sign. I took it as a chance to get the Garage door shut properly.
